# Verification — 23 November 2026

Every story on the front page was checked against live reporting on
23 Nov 2026 (AWST). All six are real; figures below are as reported on
the day. The edition is dated Monday 23 November 2026 and, like a
Monday paper, carries the weekend's news cycle: events of 20–23 Nov
that were the day's headlines on the 23rd.

## 1. Nvidia brings Huang back as CEO — TECH · SANTA CLARA

- Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated co-founder Jensen Huang as CEO,
  president and board member on Monday 23 Nov, effective immediately —
  11 days after he resigned following revelations of at least two
  extramarital affairs, one with a woman the US government suspects to
  be a Chinese spy. (Reuters, Wall Street Journal, CNN, TechCrunch)
- The board said it had "received input from senior levels of the
  United States government" and was "confident that Mr. Huang's
  reinstatement poses no national security risk." (WSJ, Investopedia)
- President Trump said on Truth Social and on CNBC that the board made
  "the correct and proper decision", that the US government had no
  evidence Huang engaged in unlawful conduct, and that his own
  intervention was "very important". He said he spoke to Huang on
  Sunday night. (Reuters, WSJ)
- Shares leapt 33% in early trading (Reuters; Investopedia: up 30%),
  the Nasdaq rallied 6% and the S&P 500 rose 4%, recovering Friday's
  plunge. Interim CEO Colette Kress remains CFO. (Reuters, Investopedia)

## 2. Iran's tanker gambit tests US blockade — CONFLICT · HORMUZ

- Tehran is preparing to send 14 oil tankers through the US naval
  blockade, according to a report by Hamshahri (Tehran-municipality
  outlet) citing preparations at Kharg Island. (Gulf News live blog,
  23 Nov)
- Maritime intelligence firm Windward AI identified at least 31 laden,
  sanctioned tankers already positioned beyond the Strait of Hormuz
  between 13 and 20 Nov; four are crude carriers holding an estimated
  4.9 million barrels. The closest, Suezmax TOUR 2, sits off Chabahar,
  three to six hours' sailing from the blockade line. (Gulf News)
- The Joint Maritime Information Centre said seven oil tankers have
  been hit since 16 Nov, including three struck near Oman's coast on
  20–21 Nov. (Gulf News)
- Bahrain's Interior Ministry said an Iranian attack on Saturday hit a
  civilian industrial facility (smoke over Sitra Island, home to
  Bapco's refinery), injuring two people — one Bahraini and one
  Pakistani, one in critical condition. Bahrain's air defences
  intercepted several Iranian aerial attacks. The IRGC said it struck
  US positions at the Sheikh Isa / Al-Riffa and Juffair / Awali bases.
  (Gulf News, CGTN, Manila Times, Just Security)
- The IRGC said its air defences shot down a US MQ-1 drone east of the
  Strait of Hormuz on Sunday, and its navy said two tankers caught fire
  after striking mines north of Qeshm Island. (CGTN, Gulf News)
- Trump said he is inclined to resume military strikes against Iran
  after the New Year if negotiations fail. (Yeni Safak, 23 Nov)

## 3. US offers Manila $2.4B defence deal — with a catch — DIPLOMACY · MANILA

- The White House announced, after Trump's state visit to Manila
  (17–19 Nov, closing the 49th ASEAN Summit), a package of more than
  $2 billion in proposed Foreign Military Financing loans plus $400
  million in military financing. (SCMP, 23 Nov; BusinessWorld, 19 Nov;
  Philstar, 19 Nov)
- The $400M is for long-range fires, command-and-control systems,
  critical infrastructure and multi-domain drones, "to expand its
  operational reach and deterrence capabilities in the South China
  Sea". (BusinessWorld, Philstar)
- The catch, per analysts: the loan binds Manila to buy defence
  materiel exclusively from American suppliers, and much of the funding
  had already been approved under previous commitments. The loan would
  support more than 7,000 US jobs, per the White House fact sheet.
  (SCMP, BusinessWorld)
- The package is a proposal, not a completed agreement: terms and
  timetable undisclosed, subject to US congressional funding.
  (The Library Estate, 23 Nov)

## 4. SpaceX sets 32-hour pad turnaround record — SPACE · VANDENBERG

- SpaceX launched Falcon 9 / NROL-136 from Vandenberg SLC-4E at
  4:23 a.m. PST on Sunday 22 Nov, just 32 hours, 3 minutes and
  21 seconds after USSF-317 left the same pad at 8:20 p.m. PST on
  20 Nov — the fastest pad turnaround in SpaceX history, beating the
  previous 45-hour record set at Pad 40 in Florida by about 13 hours.
  (technology.org, Space.com via Everything Space, NewsBytes)
- NROL-136 was the NRO's sixth launch of 2026 (all on Falcon 9) and
  the 15th launch for its proliferated satellite architecture. Booster
  B1097 flew for the 14th time. (technology.org, NewsBytes)
- Reported as a record on 23 Nov. The Ariane 6 launch of 36 Amazon Leo
  satellites from Kourou, scheduled for 23 Nov, was postponed to no
  earlier than 24 Nov after a ground-support technical issue — noted
  here, not used. (Arianespace newsroom)

## 5. Four nations to host 2031 Women's World Cup — SPORT · ZURICH

- FIFA's Extraordinary Congress in Zurich on Monday 23 Nov appointed
  the United States, Mexico, Costa Rica and Jamaica as hosts of the
  2031 FIFA Women's World Cup, and England, Northern Ireland, Scotland
  and Wales as hosts of 2035 — both by acclamation, both unopposed.
  (FIFA, Reuters, AP, CNN, US Soccer)
- 2031 is the first senior Women's World Cup across four countries and
  the first with 48 teams and 104 matches; the bid offers 20 cities
  (14 in the US, four in Mexico, plus San José and Kingston).
  (US Soccer, AP, Reuters)
- 2035 is the first Women's World Cup staged in the United Kingdom;
  England hosts a senior World Cup for the first time since 1966.
  (AP, Reuters)

## 6. Mexico captures 'Chapo Isidro' — CRIME · SINALOA

- Mexican special forces captured Fausto Isidro Meza-Flores, alias
  "Chapo Isidro", in a stealth raid on Friday night (20 Nov) in
  Guasave, Sinaloa, with US intelligence support; he was flown to
  Mexico City on Saturday. (WSJ, CNN, Daily Star, Manila Times)
- Meza-Flores, 44, led the Meza-Flores Transnational Criminal
  Organization (Beltrán Leyva-affiliated, allied with a Sinaloa cartel
  faction), controlling smuggling routes in Sonora for fentanyl and
  other narcotics. He was on the FBI's 10 Most Wanted list with a
  $5 million reward, indicted in the US since 2012. (WSJ, CNN, FBI
  via Geo.tv)
- The raid left four soldiers and seven gunmen dead (11 killed overall
  in the ensuing violence); eight arrested including his No. 2 and
  financial operator. A professional baseball game at Kuroda Park was
  suspended as helicopters fired overhead. (CNN, Arab News, Manila
  Times)
- Still headline news on 23 Nov (Manila Times, 23 Nov edition).
