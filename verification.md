## Concept — "The Atlas of a Day"

The news as geography: one day, pinned to the map. The page is organised
around a world map — the six lead stories are numbered red pins on it, and
every other story is an amber dot. A UTC timeline runs under the masthead,
a numbers band counts the day, a legend explains how sure each story is,
and the remaining 29 stories run as a compact three-column register.
No grid of equal cards (the 22 Nov edition) and no broadsheet lead story
(the early 23 Nov "Handover") — the map is the hierarchy. Slate ink on
pale paper, teal and amber accents, red only for the pins. This is a fresh
concept of its own, per the standing anti-repetition (腻) rule.

## Labels — HOW SURE
